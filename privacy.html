Privacy policy

Garmin Training Hub · Updated 26 October 2026

Personal use and purpose

Garmin Training Hub is a personal application maintained by chefrosiers-blip. It is intended solely for the owner's own training analysis and is currently being configured.

Data processed

The application retrieves the owner's authorised Garmin activity and wellness data. Depending on the enabled features, this may include exercise records, timestamps, distance, heart rate, location, sleep and recovery data.

Google access and storage

Google OAuth authorisation is used to create and manage training exports and summaries in the owner's Google Drive and Google Sheets. The application requests the drive.file scope, which limits access to files created by or explicitly authorised for this application.

Synchronisation is designed to run in the owner's Google Cloud project. Credentials are stored separately from training exports and must not be placed in this public website or its source repository.

Use and disclosure

Data is used for personal synchronisation, storage and training analysis. The application does not sell data, use it for advertising, or publish it on this website. Garmin and Google process data as necessary to provide their respective services.

The application's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Training exports remain in the owner's Google account until deleted by the owner. To stop further collection, disable the scheduled synchronisation and revoke the application's Google access. The Garmin Personal API key can also be revoked in Garmin Connect.

Revoking access does not automatically delete existing exports. Delete those separately from Google Drive and Google Sheets, including their trash where applicable. Google Cloud logs and backups follow their configured retention settings.

This public website

This website contains project information only and does not receive Garmin records or OAuth credentials. It includes no application-added analytics or advertising trackers. GitHub Pages may process visitor information, including IP addresses, for hosting and security under GitHub's privacy statement.

Contact

Contact the maintainer through the GitHub profile. Do not post credentials, health information or private training records in public issues.
